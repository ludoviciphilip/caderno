# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness,
being respectful of differing opinions and experiences, and giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private
harassment, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project owner via a
private message on GitHub (e.g. opening a confidential security advisory or contacting
[@ludoviciphilip](https://github.com/ludoviciphilip) directly). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.

For the full text of the Contributor Covenant v2.1, see
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
